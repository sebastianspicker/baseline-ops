### Key Risk Areas

1. **External.psm1** has 18 exported functions and zero tests. It contains the `Invoke-NativeCommand` function with an explicit command-injection guard that is completely untested.
2. **Evidence.psm1** has security-critical path-traversal and quota checks in `Copy-ToEvidence` with zero tests.
